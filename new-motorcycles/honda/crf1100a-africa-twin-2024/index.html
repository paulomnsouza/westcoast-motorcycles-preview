Overview

About the CRF1100A Africa Twin

  • 1084cc liquid-cooled Unicam® parallel-twin engine
  • Apple CarPlay® & Android Auto™
  • Tubeless tyres

New adjustable rally windscreen

Long stretches of open road and dusty rugged trails are where the Africa Twin feels most at home. Inspired by Honda’s Dakar Rally success, the iconic adventure tourer combines a powerful engine and compact chassis to deliver a smooth, controlled ride on both highways and dirt roads.

Adventure ready

The compact chassis, adjustable windscreen and tubeless tyres keep you ready for off-road trails and long highway rides.

High performance

The 1084cc twin-cylinder engine delivers powerful mid-range torque with revised valve timing and increased compression.

Handling

Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), selectable ABS with cornering technology, multiple riding modes, cruise control and 6-axis inertial measurement unit (IMU) controlled ABS.

  • Electronic Suspension, 6.5inch display, Apple
